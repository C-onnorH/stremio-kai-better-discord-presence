# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release.
- Discord status reads "Watching <title>" (movies and shows).
- Poster as the large image, progress bar while playing, "Paused" when paused.
- Shows display the episode title and season/episode (e.g. "The Ghost of Harrenhal · S2 E5"), looked up on Cinemeta.
- "View on IMDb" button.
- Settings file: `portable_config/script-opts/discord_presence.conf`.
- `test-discord.bat` to check the Discord connection on its own.
